# Checkpoint 1 originality, licensing, and assets

> **Last updated:** 2026-08-17  
> **Status:** Preliminary review only; legal clearance not performed

## Identity construction

All three candidate symbols are path-only, code-native studies in the exact source
commit. No external logo, live SVG text, filter, gradient, raster mark, or generated
identity image is embedded in the candidates. Each remains a preliminary vector and
working name, not a production master asset.

The candidates intentionally avoid generic leaf, shield, heart, checkmark, and
nutrition-badge marks. Preliminary originality review can identify obvious resemblance;
it cannot establish trademark availability, design-right clearance, or non-infringement.

## Benchmark boundary

[The benchmark report](../../phase5/PHASE5A2_DIRECTION_BENCHMARKS.md) extracts hierarchy,
provenance, comparison, scanner-state, permission, and adaptive-layout principles from
current first-party sources. It explicitly rejects copying benchmark identity,
taxonomies, layouts, motion language, iconography, and trade dress.

## Typography

No proposed font is served or rendered by the review routes. The bounded transfer assay,
payload hashes, bytes, source links, OFL status, and adoption gate are recorded in
[PHASE5A2_TYPOGRAPHY_PROVENANCE.md](../../phase5/PHASE5A2_TYPOGRAPHY_PROVENANCE.md).

The assay is not adoption approval. Complete message-corpus coverage, immutable upstream
commit/license capture, measured fallback metrics, proposed-font CLS, production budget,
security, and legal review remain required after selection.

## Imagery, illustration, and icons

- No third-party photography, illustration, texture, or candidate icon pack is adopted.
- Current package/product specimens are CSS and authored vector-path review studies.
- No AI-generated image is part of the final candidate evidence.
- Production crop, fallback, loading, error, attribution, dark-mode, and performance
  rules remain to be specified for the selected Golden Reference direction.

## Clearance required after selection

1. Freeze the selected vector master and derived-asset ledger.
2. Search working name and mark through appropriate trademark/design databases.
3. Obtain human legal review for intended markets and classes.
4. Capture immutable license texts, Reserved Font Names, upstream commits, and exact
   vendored hashes for any adopted font or asset.
5. Produce and review favicon, maskable icon, social, Open Graph, splash, clear-space,
   minimum-size, and misuse evidence.

Eric's Checkpoint 1 selection is a design decision subject to these conditions; it is
not legal or production clearance.
