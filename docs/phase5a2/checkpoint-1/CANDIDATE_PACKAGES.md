# Checkpoint 1 candidate packages

> **Last updated:** 2026-08-17
> **Status:** Comparative facts; no winner selected

All candidates use the same deterministic synthetic product, evidence semantics,
surfaces, capture studies, and review-only route. Their identity systems are paired
one-to-one with their art directions for this checkpoint.

## A — Source Fold

- **Character:** warm editorial utility with a clipped package-label fold and visible
  source-to-evidence progression.
- **Hierarchy:** package source and narrative lead into observed, calculated,
  contextualized, and decision layers.
- **Identity evidence:** path-only mark, primary TryVit lockup, dark, monochrome, micro,
  and maskable studies.
- **Typography proposal:** Manrope with sparse Source Serif 4 emphasis; the prototype
  renders only the existing system stack.
- **Strength to examine:** food/package relevance and an ownable provenance metaphor.
- **Risk to examine:** source storytelling can delay the decision signal or make dense
  application work less immediate.

## B — Evidence Register

- **Character:** precise ruled register with explicit record, source, and evidence
  notation.
- **Hierarchy:** the strongest emphasis is on comparable rows, status, and auditability.
- **Identity evidence:** path-only open-register mark, primary TryVit lockup, dark,
  monochrome, micro, and maskable studies.
- **Typography proposal:** IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono; the prototype renders only
  the existing V2 system stacks.
- **Strength to examine:** operational information density and evidence separation.
- **Risk to examine:** institutional/document conventions may feel less food-specific or
  visually ownable.

## C — Open Core

- **Character:** spacious decision-first composition with nested evidence depth and an
  intentionally open geometric core.
- **Hierarchy:** decision, evidence, context, and source open in successive layers.
- **Identity evidence:** path-only segmented mark, primary TryVit lockup, dark,
  monochrome, micro, and maskable studies.
- **Typography proposal:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next with Newsreader emphasis; the
  prototype renders only the existing system stacks.
- **Strength to examine:** editorial distinction and an explicit read-deeper model.
- **Risk to examine:** large display typography and abstract geometry can reduce mobile
  density or food-category recognition.

## Shared implemented surfaces

Each candidate implements identity, landing, authenticated home, product/evidence,
scanner, and motion-study surfaces. The frozen evidence retains the same seven still
studies and two recordings for every candidate. The interactive contract additionally
supports EN/PL/DE, light/dark, full/reduced motion, bounded surface states, keyboard
operation, forced colors, text spacing, and reflow profiles.
