These are Checkpoint 1 path-selection scores. They do not establish passage of the

The reviewers agree that:

- Source Fold is the strongest identity and most category-relevant mark.
- Evidence Register has the strongest evidence ordering and compact product mechanics.
- Open Core has material category-confusion and micro-mark risks.
- accessibility, native language, legal, real-device, and production performance
  approval remain outside this packet.

They disagree on the master art direction. Reviewer A chooses Evidence Register for
clarity, responsive density, and auditability. Reviewer B chooses Source Fold for
identity coherence, category fit, and ownability. That disagreement is useful and is
not averaged away.

## Primary-agent recommendation

- **Art direction: B — Evidence Register**
- **Identity: A — Source Fold**
- **Typography: retain the system stack through the first Golden Reference iteration**

The recommendation gives product decision support priority in the art-direction choice
while following the unanimous identity result. It is a precise synthesis, not permission
to combine arbitrary visual traits:

1. Preserve B's observed/derived/context/unknown evidence order, score visibility,
   compact responsive density, and explicit record states.
2. Rebuild the visual identity layer around A's folded package/source mark and provenance
   metaphor.
3. Establish one coherent palette, type hierarchy, wordmark casing, grid, and mark-use
   system before producing the six Golden References; do not paste A's logo into an
   otherwise unchanged cobalt ledger.
