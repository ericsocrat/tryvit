# Product positioning — TryVit

Decision: evidence-first private beta for everyday shoppers in PL and DE.
See [the release ledger](implementation/EVIDENCE_FIRST_REBUILD.md) for rollout
status, and [the evidence policy](EVIDENCE_DATA_POLICY.md) for precise boundaries.

## Job to be done

When shopping for food, help a person find the right product, understand what its
recorded information supports and what remains unknown, inspect compatible
differences, and retrieve products they saved.

The central experience is Home, Find, Scan, Saved and Compare. Categories support
finding products rather than duplicating the primary journey. Recipes, history
and other secondary tools must earn their complexity and cannot depend on
unsupported health grades.

## What is useful even without a score

- Clear product identity, market and source image when available.
- Nutrition with unit, measurement basis, qualifiers and evidence state.
- Positive allergen evidence prominently displayed; unknown is not absence.
- Traceable source observations and their actual retrieval/update dates.
- Neutral comparisons only where the observations are compatible.
- Reliable personal collections, notes and retrieval without fabricated success.

An explainable heuristic is not necessarily a valid model. TryVit therefore
retires the former universal aggregate from consumer use rather than advertising
its transparency as scientific validation.

## Deliberate non-goals

No diagnosis, treatment advice, personal dietary prescription or allergen-safety
certification. No promise of exhaustive market coverage, live price/availability,
or package verification without evidence. No replacement scoring formula is
required for this release. No new paid infrastructure or public-signup launch is
implicit in the rebuild.

## Open assumptions

Demand, repeat use, willingness to pay, monetization and the comparative value of
individual secondary features remain unvalidated. A passing test suite or a
polished interface does not validate them. Assess them with separately scoped
private-beta user evidence rather than feature volume.
