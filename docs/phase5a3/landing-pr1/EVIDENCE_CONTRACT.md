# Phase 5A.3 landing evidence contract

The media in this directory is review evidence, not an accepted production baseline.
Every retained local-capture media file and the motion observation are hashed in
`evidence/manifest.json` and bound to source commit
`1f7ad2c0e52833f06a3d17e010ad653b366ee291`.

## Stills

The exact-head guarded harness captures:

- EN at 390×844, 768×1024, and 1440×900 in light and dark modes;
- 320×900 reflow;
- a 640 CSS-pixel layout representing a 1280-pixel desktop viewport at 200% browser
  zoom;
- forced colors at 390×844;
- Polish at 390×844 with the WCAG text-spacing override;
- long German copy at 1440×900 in dark mode;
- JavaScript-disabled EN at 390×844 in light and dark modes.

The 200% artifact records the standards-relevant effective CSS viewport rather than
browser chrome: 1280 physical/CSS pixels at 100% becomes 640 CSS pixels at 200%. The
separate 320-pixel capture proves the stricter reflow case.

## Motion

The normal and reduced-motion WebM recordings traverse the same content journey through
the final-action section; they do not show the footer. Reduced motion removes
non-essential transition movement without removing meaning or interaction.
`landing-motion-performance.json` records zero layout shift,
zero long tasks, and zero animation-attributable tasks over 50 ms for the measured
interaction window.

Both recordings are generated by the repository-pinned Playwright Chromium harness.
They must be reviewed from beginning to end rather than sampled from thumbnails.

## Behavioral proof

`npm run phase5:landing-review` runs 20 guarded tests and proves:

- full-page Axe using WCAG 2 A/AA and 2.1 A/AA tags with no exclusions or disabled
  rules;
- first-tab skip navigation, main-landmark focus, and the next evidence link;
- complete server-rendered meaning with JavaScript disabled;
- no landing backend, feature-flag, image, or WOFF2 traffic;
- no candidate font loading on `/auth/login`;
- horizontal reflow bounds for every still;
- exact project locale for EN, PL, and DE.

The automated Polish assertion proves horizontal containment. Original-resolution visual
review found no observed overlap, but the harness does not geometrically prove absence
of every possible occlusion.

The motion performance observation covers the measured narrative interaction window,
not the complete recorded scroll/theme sequence.

The recordings are initiated from a 390×844 page viewport; Playwright's encoded video
content area is 368×800. The filenames retain the requested page viewport while the
encoded dimensions are disclosed here.

This is browser automation evidence, not assistive-technology, real-device, or native
language approval.

The separately generated authoritative Linux baseline candidates are hashed and bound
in `evidence/linux-candidate.json`; they are not accepted production baselines.
