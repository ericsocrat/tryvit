## Selected

- **Sweets (PL)** — Highest refreshed score, with 225 safely enrichable products, strong explicit allergen evidence, and high barcode/search relevance.
- **Frozen & Prepared (DE)** — High-value prepared-food scope with 98.0% ingredient-source completeness; ambiguous tokens are bounded and withheld by governance.
- **Dairy (PL)** — Second-highest refreshed score, with 199 safely enrichable products and explicit allergen evidence for nearly the entire evidence-bearing batch.
- **Meat (DE)** — High everyday-search relevance, 96.4% source completeness, 188 safely enrichable products, and only one ambiguous token.

## Leading deferred

- **Snacks (DE)** — Ranks fourth, but Meat DE was selected for its larger product gain and materially smaller ambiguity queue (1 token versus 18), keeping this batch lower risk.
- **Instant & Frozen (DE)** — High linkage potential but the largest ambiguous-token queue among the leading candidates increases review and regression risk.
- **Bread (DE)** — Excellent source and allergen completeness, but it is a smaller gain than the selected categories and can anchor the next controlled batch.
- **Spreads & Dips (DE)** — Meaningful gain, but lower source completeness and a larger ambiguity queue than Meat DE.
- **Drinks (PL)** — Strong barcode relevance but only 32 products have explicit allergen evidence, so it provides less balanced quality gain than the selected scopes.
- **Bread (PL)** — High user relevance, but only 61.4% of missing products have ingredient source evidence.
